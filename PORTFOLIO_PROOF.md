# ECA-1 — Portfolio Proof Contract

**Track:** Research / embodied cognitive architecture

ECA-1 is complete only when embodied cognitive capabilities are demonstrated in controlled environments with independent verification, robustness testing, and clear separation between architecture intent and measured behavior.

Required proof: executable perception/state/action loops; tests for sensor uncertainty, memory/state, planning, tool/actuator boundaries, and verification; adversarial environments, sensor corruption, delayed feedback, actuator/tool failure, and recovery; benchmarks for task success, latency, sample efficiency, generalization, calibration, and recovery; sandbox/authority controls; reproducible experiments and limitations.

**Next proof target:** define a closed simulated/robotic task suite with unfamiliar variations and injected sensor/action failures, then score independent task completion and transfer rather than self-reported success.
